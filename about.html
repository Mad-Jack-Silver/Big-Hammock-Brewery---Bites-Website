The Big Hammock Story

Brewed with Passion in Ocala

Discover how a small team of passionate brewers and culinarians created Ocala's most unique downtown gathering place.

Our Origin

Where Great Beer Meets Real Flavor

When we opened our doors at 103 SE 1st Ave in downtown Ocala, Florida, we wanted to break away from traditional brewpub predictability. We asked ourselves: why shouldn't a community craft brewery serve food that is just as thrilling and bold as the beer in the glass?

Instead of standard frozen burgers and deep-fried bar fare, we built a scratch kitchen centered around the extraordinary comfort of Japanese street food—rich, slow-simmered ramen broths, warm fluffy steamed bao buns, crispy potstickers, and Asian-fusion pressed baguettes.

Every morning, our kitchen begins simmering 16-hour bone broths, roasting heritage pork bellies with sweet soy glazes, and prepping fresh scallions and seasonal vegetables, while our brew tanks hum right behind the bar.

Our Core Ethos

What Guides Everything We Do

From ingredient sourcing to the hospitality at your table.

Zero Shortcuts

We don't use powdered broth bases or artificial extracts. Authentic tonkotsu and miso ramen takes hours of patience, real marrow bones, and delicate aromatics.

Downtown Community First

We are proud to be an anchor of Ocala's downtown revitalized core. A welcoming space for families, craft beer aficionados, tourists, and hardworking locals.

Creative Innovation

Whether it's introducing Marion County to broth-less Mazemen noodles or dry-hopping a fruited guava kettle sour, we believe in exciting your tastebuds.

The Atmosphere

A Place to Unwind in Downtown Ocala

Whether you pull up a stool at the handcrafted taproom bar, gather around a communal table with friends, or relax outdoors under the Florida sky on our patio, Big Hammock is designed to feel like home.
