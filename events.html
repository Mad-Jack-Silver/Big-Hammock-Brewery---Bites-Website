Weekly Taproom Happenings

Mark your calendars for our signature weekly events and community gatherings.

Every Tuesday 7:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Trivia Tuesday

Gather your smartest friends for general trivia with great prizes! 1st, 2nd, and 3rd place teams win Big Hammock gift cards and brewery merchandise. Enjoy $13 custom tasting flights all evening.

Free to Play • All Ages Welcome
Every Wednesday All Day

Bao & Brew Night

Midweek flavor boost! Get $2.00 off any trio of steamed bao buns (Chashu Pork, Crispy Karaage Chicken, or Pot Roast) when paired with any draft pint.

Dine-In Special Only
Every Thursday 6:30 PM – 9:00 PM

Thirsty Thursday & Acoustics

Relax to live acoustic sessions from talented local Central Florida musicians on the patio. Enjoy $5 select core pints and $3 off all 32oz crowler fills to take home for the weekend.

Live Music & Discount Pours
Weekends 11:00 AM – 10:00 PM

Chef's Special Weekend Tappings

Every Friday, our kitchen debuts limited-run ramen creations and appetizer specials, paired with new micro-batch experimental beers tapped fresh from our fermenters.
