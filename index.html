The BHB Experience

Craft Beer Culture Meets Asian Street Food

Located in historic downtown Ocala at 103 SE 1st Ave, Big Hammock Brewery & Bites was born from a passion for boundary-pushing brewing and unforgettable Asian comfort food.

We believe nothing complements a crisp, hop-forward IPA or rich nitro porter quite like the complex, savory depths of authentic Japanese ramen, succulent bao buns, and zesty Asian street appetizers.

Scratch Kitchen

Broths simmered for 16+ hours and pork roasted daily.

Small-Batch Taps

Fresh ales, lagers, sours, and seasonal flights.

Outdoor Patio & Bar

Relaxed downtown vibe with friendly table service.

Crowlers To-Go

32oz fresh-canned craft beer sealed cold for home.
